# Scoring Rubric

Score each generated result out of 100.

## Categories

- Geometry / factual fidelity: 30
- Style stability: 20
- Layout and information hierarchy: 15
- Text and label hygiene: 15
- Image role separation: 10
- Professional finish: 10

## Veto Flags

Any veto means the result is not production-ready regardless of total score:

- Key geographic, plan, satellite, photo, or rendering geometry changed.
- Reference watermark, logo, text, project content, or recognizable scene copied.
- Output type is wrong.
- Critical Chinese labels are gibberish, foreign-language, or misleading.

## Score Interpretation

- 90-100: production candidate; only minor polish.
- 75-89: usable draft; needs targeted prompt revision.
- 60-74: weak draft; template needs stronger constraints.
- Below 60: failure; reclassify template or split task into staged workflow.
