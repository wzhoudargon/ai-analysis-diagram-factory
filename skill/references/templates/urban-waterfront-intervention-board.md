# urban-waterfront-intervention-board

## use_when
Use for city satellite maps that should become a waterfront ecological restoration and urban-interface intervention competition board.

## input_roles
- Evidence image: urban satellite map, only source for city fabric, roads, river, shoreline.
- Style references: analysis/collage/parametric board mood only.

## geometry_lock
Remove original labels but keep city grain, roads, water, shoreline, bridges, and river orientation exactly.

## style_rules
Gray-white base map, dark water, fluorescent green ecological intervention bands, black analysis lines, parametric curves, vector overlays, mixed collage diagrams.

## layout_rules
Left-heavy board. Large satellite/map base on left; right side for analysis modules, parametric generation diagram, shoreline typology evolution, vegetation/system diagrams, and bottom urban waterfront collage.

## text_rules
Only sparse English labels such as ANALYSIS, STRATEGY, SYSTEM. No paragraphs or fake text.

## prompt_template
```text
Generate a landscape architecture competition board from the uploaded urban satellite image.
Remove all original map text while preserving urban fabric, roads, water, and riverbank morphology.
Theme: linear ecological restoration and urban interface reconstruction along the waterfront, using multi-point intervention, system connection, and phased renewal.
Composition: left side dominant satellite-derived base with riverbank transformed into a continuous green intervention system; from the right side, draw multiple black curving flows connecting city fabric to the waterfront; place modular rectangular intervention zones along the shoreline.
Right modules: location analysis, parametric geometry/density analysis, urban structure decomposition, linear generation diagram, shoreline type evolution sections, vegetation dot matrices, system icons.
Bottom: grayscale plus selective color collage of industry, bridges, urban edge, and waterfront public space.
Use ultra-thin lines, parametric curves, transparent overlays, high density, sharp print quality, and competition-board hierarchy.
```

## negative_rules
No changed shoreline, no invented river, no copied labels, no random green zones detached from water, no decorative poster, no blurry text.

## failure_modes
- Satellite image remains raw and unstyled.
- Green intervention floats without city-river logic.
- Right-side modules lack hierarchy.

## revision_policy
If the board lacks structure, strengthen left/right layout and named module sequence before changing style.
