# biodiversity-ecology-board

## use_when
Use for ecological restoration, waterfront, wetland, or biodiversity boards that combine section-perspective, remediation mechanisms, planting systems, and species taxonomy.

## input_roles
- Evidence image: ecological site scene/map/photo to analyze topography, water, vegetation, and structures.
- Style references: post-digital collage, pastel palette, paper texture, ecological board language.

## geometry_lock
Derive topography, water morphology, vegetation zones, and visible structures from evidence. Do not invent unrelated habitats.

## style_rules
Post-digital architectural collage, flat painting plus soft 3D, high-key pastel sage/olive/beige/hazy blue, saturated accent only on focal circulation/path elements, aged paper grain and watercolor edges.

## layout_rules
Portrait 90x120 board. Top 40% macro section-perspective. Middle 40% three-part analysis: intervention nodes, core circular mechanism, plant system. Bottom 20% taxonomy sequence.

## text_rules
Use concise labels for species, mechanisms, and modules. Scientific/common names can be included when plausible; otherwise use habitat-generic labels.

## prompt_template
```text
Generate a portrait ecological restoration competition board from the uploaded site image.
Analyze the image first and reconstruct its real topography, water boundaries, vegetation communities, and visible artificial structures.
Top: massive section-perspective showing above-ground site conditions and below-ground soil strata, roots, groundwater, rainfall, and wind.
Middle left: 2-3 actionable intervention strategy nodes based on site conditions.
Middle core: concentric remediation mechanism diagram showing plant roots, soil/water interaction, and nutrient or pollutant transformation.
Middle right: plant system configuration with canopy, shrub, and herb layers.
Bottom: two rows of biodiversity taxonomy diagrams for target birds, fish, insects, or amphibians suitable for the habitat.
Style: bright desaturated pastel post-digital collage on fine paper, soft watercolor edges, matte finish, high detail but airy.
```

## negative_rules
No unrelated species, no fantasy ecology, no overfilled text, no glossy realism, no hard digital edges, no fake scientific claims when evidence is weak.

## failure_modes
- Species and remediation logic become arbitrary.
- Board loses three-tier structure.
- Accent colors overtake the pastel system.

## revision_policy
If facts are uncertain, make labels generic and mark inferred habitat types instead of naming exact species.
