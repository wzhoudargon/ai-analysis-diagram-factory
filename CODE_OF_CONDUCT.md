# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want this project to be useful for designers, researchers, students, and tool builders who create architecture, landscape, and urban design diagrams.

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, assume good intent, and keep discussions focused on improving the project.

## Expected Behavior

- Be specific and constructive when reporting problems.
- Respect different design workflows and tool preferences.
- Share reusable lessons without exposing private client work, private prompts, confidential images, or personal data.
- Credit external references when relevant.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private project information, secrets, credentials, or client material.
- Submitting copyrighted prompts, images, or documents that you do not have permission to share.
- Repeated off-topic promotion or spam.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, issues, pull requests, or contributions that violate this code of conduct. Repeated or severe violations may result in being blocked from the repository.

## Reporting

Report concerns by opening an issue with minimal public detail, or by contacting the maintainer through their GitHub profile.
